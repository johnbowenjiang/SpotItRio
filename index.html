Why the construction works

Use the projective plane PG(2,7). Its 57 points become symbols and its 57 lines become cards. Each line contains 8 points, so every card has 8 symbols. Any two distinct projective lines meet in exactly one point, so every two cards share exactly one symbol.

We construct 49 cards of the form y = mx + b over arithmetic modulo 7, 7 vertical cards x = a, and one line-at-infinity card. Symbols are the 49 affine points plus 8 points at infinity.
